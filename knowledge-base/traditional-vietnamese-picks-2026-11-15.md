## Interpretation

- **Hà Nội:** all 5 picks are Michelin-listed, so they pass even without a verified Google figure (128, 129) or below 4.3 (130).
- **Phú Quốc:** only 4 places qualify as clearly traditional. Si's Crab House (4.3, 1,826) passes the rating rule, but Wanderlog tags it American/bakery, so it is left out. Huong Bien (4.7, 794) is the closest miss.
- **Rejected:** Bún quậy Kiến Xây 4.2, Bánh Canh Phụng 4.1, Xin Chào 4.2, Eat Pray Love (4.2 on a second source), Xới Cơm (Đống Đa, outside the area).
- **Caveats:** none of the Phú Quốc places could be confirmed open for Nov 2026, and Vịnh Xanh appears only on Wanderlog. Check before going.
